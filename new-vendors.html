Who we are

Facility management, done through local crews

Every building we serve has a signed service agreement with City Wide and a Facility Solutions Manager who owns that relationship. We do not run crews. We coordinate the work through vendors like you.

What we do

We sell and manage commercial facility services for office buildings, medical, schools, warehouses and industrial sites. The client signs with us. We handle the sales, the contract, the scope, the inspections and the billing.

How we do it

Each property gets a Facility Solutions Manager who walks the building, writes a scope that makes sense, sets the price, and stays on the account. When the client needs something, they call us, not you.

Where you fit

You are the expert in your trade. You do the work under your own business name, with your own people and equipment. We manage the client and the contract. You invoice City Wide, never the client.

How you get paid

One invoice a month. A set pay date.

Most crews who have done commercial work know the feeling of finishing the job and then chasing the check for weeks. Working with City Wide, the schedule is set and the conditions are in your control.

Send one invoice a month covering every building you service. If it reaches us by noon on the 1st, you are paid on or around the 10th. An invoice that comes in after noon on the 1st is paid on or around the 25th, without exception.

Your monthly pay for each building is agreed in writing before you start, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

What it costs to join

Nothing. There is no fee.

Nobody at City Wide will ask you for money to get on file or to be considered for work. Getting on file is paperwork and compliance, nothing more. What you do need is what any commercial contractor already carries:

  • A business bank account in your company name. We pay businesses, not individuals.
  • General liability insurance with the certificate holder reading exactly: CAVU NC LLC dba City Wide Facility Solutions, 2300 W Meadowview Road, Suite 107/109, Greensboro, NC 27407.
  • Workers' compensation, even if it's just you. North Carolina only requires it at three or more employees, but City Wide requires it for every subcontractor regardless of size.
  • A W-9 in your exact business name.
  • Any license your trade requires in North Carolina.

Avoid the delays

The five things that slow vendors down

Nearly every onboarding delay is one of these. Dodge them all and you are on file and eligible for work in days.

  1. A personal name on the banking. The check or bank letter needs your company name. We pay businesses, not individuals.
  2. Names that almost match. "Piedmont Cleaning" on the check and "Piedmont Cleaning Group LLC" on the packet sends it back to you. Letter for letter, everywhere.
  3. No workers' comp. Even a one-person crew needs it to work City Wide buildings. Ask your agent early; it is usually the longest wait.
  4. A certificate naming the wrong holder. "City Wide" alone is not enough. Your agent needs the exact certificate holder shown above.
  5. Starter checks and stale letters. Temporary checks, screenshots, and bank letters older than 90 days cannot be accepted.
