Submit Your Monthly Invoice

Already have an invoice from your own system? Email it in. Need one? Build it right here. Either way it goes straight to City Wide of The Triad Accounts Payable. Invoices are due by noon on the 1st of the month.

DEADLINE

Invoices are due by noon on the 1st. Invoices received on time are paid on or around the 10th. Any invoice received after noon on the 1st is paid on or around the 25th, without exception. Certain high-ticket projects carry a pay-when-paid stipulation, which is stated on the job.

How would you like to submit your invoice?

Both go straight to City Wide of The Triad Accounts Payable.

Email your invoice

This opens a new email to cwtriadinvoices@gocitywide.com with the subject filled in. Attach your invoice PDF or photo, then hit send.

Email not opening? Send your invoice to cwtriadinvoices@gocitywide.com with your company name and month of service in the subject.

Before you start: have your contract amounts handy, and the Work Order number from your City Wide contact for any extra charge or project work. Lines without a Work Order number can slow down payment.
